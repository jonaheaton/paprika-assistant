# Item Categorization and Expiration Guidelines

## Expiration Date Heuristics

Calculate expiration dates from the **delivery date** (shown in the order header), not today's date.

**Produce:**
- Leafy greens (lettuce, spinach, bok choy): 7 days
- Berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries): 14 days
- Bananas: 7 days
- Citrus fruits: 21 days
- Other fruits (apples, kiwi, mango): 14 days
- Root vegetables (carrots, potatoes, shallots, onions): 21 days
- Other vegetables (cucumber, peppers, zucchini): 14 days

**Dairy:**
- Milk: 10 days
- Butter: 30 days
- Cheese (soft — cream cheese, ricotta, fresh mozzarella): 14 days
- Cheese (hard/shredded — parmesan, cheddar, gouda): 30 days
- Yogurt: 14 days
- Eggs: 30 days
- Tofu: 14 days

**Bakery:**
- Bread: 10 days
- Pastries: 7 days

**Frozen Foods:**
- No expiration date needed (assume long-term storage)

**Meat:**
- Fresh meat: 7 days
- Processed meat: 14 days

**Seafood:**
- Fresh seafood: 3 days
- Frozen seafood: No expiration date

**Other categories:**
- Default to no expiration date unless clearly perishable

## Item Filtering

**Skip non-food items:**
- Paper products (toilet paper, paper towels, tissues)
- Cleaning supplies
- Personal care items (shampoo, razors, scrunchies)
- Health products
- Household items (aluminum foil, plastic bags, batteries)
- Pet supplies

**Include:**
- All food items
- Beverages (including probiotic sodas, sparkling water)
- Condiments and sauces
