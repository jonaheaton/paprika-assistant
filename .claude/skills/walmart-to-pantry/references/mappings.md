# Item Categorization and Expiration Guidelines

## Paprika Aisle Mappings

Use these aisle names when adding items to Paprika pantry (exact match required):

- Produce
- Dairy
- Bakery
- Frozen Foods
- Beverages
- Sauces and Condiments
- Snacks
- Meat
- Seafood
- Baking Goods
- Oils and Dressings
- Spices and Seasonings
- Pasta, Rice and Beans
- Canned and Jar Goods
- Uncategorized

## Expiration Date Heuristics

Calculate expiration dates from purchase date (today) based on category:

**Produce:**
- Leafy greens (lettuce, spinach, bok choy): 7 days
- Berries: 14 days
- Citrus fruits: 21 days
- Other fruits (kiwi, apples): 14 days
- Root vegetables (carrots, potatoes): 21 days
- Other vegetables (cucumber, peppers): 14 days

**Dairy:**
- Milk: 10 days
- Butter: 30 days
- Cheese (soft): 14 days
- Cheese (hard): 30 days
- Yogurt: 14 days
- Tofu: 14 days

**Bakery:**
- Bread: 10 days
- Pastries: 7 days

**Frozen Foods:**
- No expiration date needed (assume long-term storage)

**Meat:**
- Fresh meat: 7 days
- Processed meat: 14 days

**Seafood:**
- Fresh seafood: 3 days
- Frozen seafood: No expiration date

**Other categories:**
- Default to no expiration date unless clearly perishable

## Item Filtering

**Skip non-food items:**
- Tissues
- Paper products
- Cleaning supplies
- Personal care items (except explicitly food-related like edible lip balm)
- Health products
- Pet supplies

**Include:**
- All food items
- Beverages
- Condiments and sauces
